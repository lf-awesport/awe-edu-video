# Cost preflight contract / Contratto preflight costi

**Candidate normative annex. Effective status inherits the parent specification (`in-review`); it becomes implementation authority only when that parent is approved.** Applicable requirement IDs: **COST-001–COST-005, PROVIDER-003**. Every normative keyword maps to those IDs.

| Area | Normative contract (EN / IT) | Applicable requirement IDs |
|---|---|---|
| Scenarios | A versioned approvable estimate MUST show `minimum` (one valid candidate/no retry), `expected` (profile counts + probable retries), and `maximum` (all authorized candidates/retries/variants/costliest fallback), with quantities, formulas, assumptions, source and confidence. / La stima versionata approvabile DEVE mostrare minimum/expected/maximum con quantità, formule, assunzioni, fonte e confidence. | COST-001 |
| Units/conversion | Native credits, currency, bytes/month, CPU/GPU time, render count and duration MUST be retained. Currency conversion MUST add rate/source/timestamp and MUST NOT replace native data; unknown MUST NOT become zero. / Unità native DEVONO restare; conversioni DEVONO aggiungere tasso/fonte/tempo e NON sostituirle; unknown NON DEVE diventare zero. | COST-001 |
| Quote/margin | Economic evidence MUST distinguish `PriceObservation`, `UnitQuote`, `BatchQuote` and per-Candidate `QuoteBinding`. Each paid line MUST bind exact request/quantity/provider/model/schema/price/unit/issued/expiry; change or expiry invalidates the binding. Margin SHOULD be line-specific; unknown paid work MUST block unless an authorized manual estimate with margin exists. / Le evidence economiche DEVONO distinguere observation, unit/batch quote e binding per Candidate; quantità e scope sono hash-bound. | COST-001, COST-005, PROVIDER-003 |
| Budget/reservation | Budgets MUST be hierarchical (project, phase, provider, variant, scene/layer) and native-unit specific; incompatible units MUST NOT be summed as spending authority. Reservation MUST atomically reserve every applicable scope via CAS/fencing or reserve none, preventing concurrent overspend. / Budget sono per unità nativa; Reservation DEVE essere atomica su tutti gli scope applicabili. | COST-002 |
| Ledger/unknown | Ledger states MUST distinguish `quoted|reserved|charged|refunded|unknown`. An uncertain charge MUST consume the maximum reserved amount until provider-backed evidence or an authorized append-only adjustment resolves it; local timeout MUST NOT imply refund. / Charge incerto DEVE consumare il massimo riservato finché evidence o adjustment autorizzato lo risolve; timeout NON implica refund. | COST-003 |
| Attribution | Reports MUST separate `historical`, `incremental`, `total-attributed` and `fixed|marginal|allocated`; cache-hit incremental may be zero while historical remains, and shared/fixed cost MUST NOT be double counted across variants. / Report DEVONO separare le categorie e NON DEVONO duplicare costi condivisi/fissi. | COST-004 |
| Local defaults | Local compute MUST estimate frames/resolution/codec/concurrency/time/CPU/GPU/RAM/temp storage and becomes money only with configured rates. Storage SHOULD propose a visible 90-day default for candidates/intermediates, with distinct retention for selected assets/renders/evidence/backups. Human labor is out of scope but review counts MAY be shown. / Compute locale DEVE stimare risorse e diventa denaro solo con tariffa; storage DOVREBBE proporre default visibile 90 giorni; lavoro umano è escluso ma i review count POSSONO apparire. | COST-001, COST-004 |
| Reconciliation | Post-run reporting MUST compare estimate/reserved/charged/refunded, duration and storage, explain variance and calibrate future models by model/duration/resolution/workstation without rewriting historical estimates. / Il consuntivo DEVE riconciliare valori, spiegare scostamenti e calibrare il futuro senza riscrivere lo storico. | COST-003, COST-004 |

Remotion licensing preflight MUST distinguish preview/diagnostic activity from chargeable successful renders, count every planned full master/variant render, and represent subscription minimums as fixed/allocated rather than duplicating them per deliverable. Rates and applicability MUST come from a current approved licence decision, never a hard-coded historical price.
